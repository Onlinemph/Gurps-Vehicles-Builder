# Eclipse Phase Spacecraft

*Four TL10 hard-SF spacecraft for GURPS-flavored Eclipse Phase games, built with the Vehicles 2e designer (load them from the preset menu on the ve2 page). No artificial gravity, no reactionless drives: every ship is a fusion reactor, a reaction drive, and remass tankage wrapped in advanced laminate.*

*House conventions: "remass tankage" uses fuel-tank formulas with hydrogen reaction mass; drives are rated in pounds of thrust (sAccel = thrust / loaded weight); reactors are catalog fusion plants with effectively unlimited fuel — endurance below is remass, not power. The 4e conversion marks these hulls HT ..fx for the hydrogen aboard; drop the f/x if you treat remass as inert propellant.*

---

The workhorse of planetary ops: a lifting-body VTOL that drops fourteen morphs from orbit to a Martian outpost and climbs back on metallic-hydrogen thrusters. Every settlement from Elysium to the TQZ perimeter sees a dozen of these a day — which is exactly why gatecrashers and smugglers love them. Unlimited reactor endurance, but only ~2.7 hours of remass: plan your burns.
