      How the builder works

      This is a streamlined, fan-made vehicle design system that produces stat blocks in the style of the GURPS Basic Set vehicle table. It is not a reproduction of any published design system — every formula is listed below, so you can house-rule anything you disagree with.

      The big idea: a weight budget

      Pick a frame capacity — the maximum loaded weight the chassis can carry. Everything you add (structure, engine, armor, fuel, seats, weapons, people, cargo) spends pounds from that budget. Stay under budget and the design is legal.

      Formulas

      • Structure weight = chassis fraction (22–40%) × frame capacity, adjusted for Lightweight/Heavy-Duty builds.
      • HP = 4 × ∛(empty weight in lbs) — the Basic Set machine formula.
      • Top speed = k × √(hp per loaded ton) × streamlining, with k per chassis (11.5 wheeled, 9 tracked, 11 airplane, …). Displacement hulls are capped at hull speed = 1.55 × √(length in feet) mph.
      • Acceleration (yds/sec²) = (hp/ton) ÷ chassis divisor. Move is shown as accel/top-speed-in-yards-per-second (mph ÷ 2).
      • SM from the Basic Set size table by length, −1 for being a long box; length is estimated as k × ∛(loaded tons) unless you override it.
      • Armor weight = surface area × facing share × DR × material density (lbs per DR per sq ft). Area ≈ k × (frame capacity)^⅔ sq ft.
      • Range = cruise speed (70% of top) × endurance. Fuel engines burn (lbs/hp·hr × rated hp × cruise fraction); electrics drain battery Wh at cruise power.
      • Hnd/SR from chassis, worsened one step at SM +6 and again at SM +9.
      • HT = 11 (+1 Heavy-Duty). An f suffix means flammable fuel (removed by a fire suppression system).
      • Cost = structure + engine + armor + seats + gear + weapons, +20% assembly, rounded to 3 significant figures.

      The GVB Library

      If you own Steve Jackson Games' discontinued GURPS Vehicle Builder program, the GVB Library toolbar button lets you load its .rep repository files right here in the browser — about 1,900 components across GURPS Vehicles 2e and its supplements. Each component is computed with the exact formulas from the data files (with its original options, access-space choices, and TL scaling) and added to your design as an equipment line. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, and no game data is distributed with this tool: you load the files you own.
